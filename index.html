India's roughly one million ASHA workers carry frontline health care to villages on paper registers, so the advice a family gets depends on which worker visits and what she remembers, and a wrong answer about a danger sign can cost a mother or a newborn her life.

Ask about a household situation. ASHA Guard answers only from the National Immunisation Schedule and the WHO danger-sign lists. If it finds a danger sign, it tells you to refer at once. If you ask for a diagnosis or a medicine dose, it blocks the question and sends you to your supervisor. Try one of the examples, or type your own.

National Immunisation Schedule

Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, Universal Immunisation Programme. JE vaccine in endemic districts only. Vitamin A doses continue every 6 months up to 5 years.

Danger signs

If you see any of these, take the mother or baby to the nearest health facility now. Do not wait for the next visit.

Rules and sources

The guard, in plain words

  • Allowlist of sources. Answers come only from the three sources below. Nothing else can be cited.
  • No dose, no diagnosis. Any request for a medicine name, a dose, or a diagnosis is blocked and sent to your supervisor (ANM or medical officer).
  • Danger signs are never gated. A danger sign gets an urgent referral at once, with no confirmation step in the way.
  • Confirm threshold. A symptom that is not a listed danger sign needs your supervisor to confirm before any guidance is given. In demo mode that confirmation is simulated.
  • Daily cap. 50 answers a day. Past the cap, the guard blocks further answers and sends you to your supervisor.
  • Ledger. Every question, answer and block is recorded with the time, the decision and the rule that applied.

Sources

  • National Immunisation Schedule, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India: nhm.gov.in, National Immunization Schedule (PDF)
  • IMNCI danger signs (WHO and UNICEF): IMNCI summary, young infant and child and neonatal danger signs as recommended by WHO and UNICEF
  • WHO Pregnancy, Childbirth, Postpartum and Newborn Care (2015), nine pregnancy danger signs: danger signs in pregnancy study citing the WHO guide
  • ASHA workforce, about 10 lakh (1 million) workers under the National Health Mission: Telegraph India and National Health Mission overview
  • Universal Immunisation Programme scale, about 27 million infants and 30 million pregnant women each year: Business Standard

Honest limits

  • This is a demo. Answers are simulated lookups from a fixed protocol set, not a live medical service.
  • It does not diagnose, does not name medicines, and does not give doses.
  • It only knows the immunisation schedule and the danger-sign lists. Anything else is refused.
  • It is not a substitute for an ANM, a medical officer, or a health facility.
  • All data stays on this device, in local storage. There are no accounts and no patient records.
